Cricket Overnight

Every morning, a short video of the international cricket played overnight: results, scores, the key performances and what is coming up next.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@CricketOvernight

How the videos are made

Each video is produced on our own computer from public match data and narrated with an AI voice. The "Cricket Overnight Uploader" app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload these videos to the Cricket Overnight channel.
