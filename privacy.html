Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy covers the "Cricket Overnight Uploader" app and this website, run by the Cricket Overnight YouTube channel.

What the app does

The app is used only by the channel owner. It uploads the channel's own daily videos to the Cricket Overnight YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, using the single permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It does not read, change or delete anything else in the Google or YouTube account.

Data we access and keep

  • When the owner signs in, Google returns an access token. The app stores the resulting token only on the owner's own computer, to upload future videos. It is never shared, sold or sent anywhere except to Google's own servers to make uploads.
  • The app does not collect, store or process data about any other person, including viewers of the channel.
  • This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes the stored access.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us through the Cricket Overnight YouTube channel.
